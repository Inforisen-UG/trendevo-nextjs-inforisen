# TrendEvo — Affordable SMM Panel in Bangladesh

Manage social media marketing orders for Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Telegram, Spotify, SoundCloud, and other platforms from one dashboard. Designed for Bangladeshi creators, businesses, freelancers, agencies, and resellers, with local payment methods and order tracking.

**URL:** https://trendevo.com

## What is TrendEvo

TrendEvo is a Bangladesh-focused social media marketing (SMM) panel that lets customers choose, order, and track services such as followers, likes, views, comments, shares, members, subscribers, and other engagement metrics. The platform supports multiple social networks, local balance top-ups, bulk ordering, reseller workflows, and an HTTP API for compatible order-management tasks. It serves individual creators, small businesses, marketing agencies, and SMM resellers.
